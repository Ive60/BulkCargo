# Privacy Policy for BulkCargo

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **BulkCargo** mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), developed by Ivo Štampalija.

## Information Collection and Use
BulkCargo is a maritime cargo calculation tool designed to operate entirely locally on your device. The Application **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data or personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers).

## Third-Party Services
The Application does not use any third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

## Data Security
All cargo figures and values you enter into the calculators within the Application are processed strictly locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.
